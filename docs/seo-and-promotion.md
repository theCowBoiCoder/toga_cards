# Toga Cards SEO and promotion

Prepared 20 November 2026. Website: https://togacards.co.uk. TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/@toga.cards (confirmed by the owner).

## Website implementation

- Unique homepage, catalogue and card titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and social previews.
- Server-rendered individual pages for published, priced cards. Sold-out listings remain reachable; unpublished checklist entries return 404.
- Product/breadcrumb JSON-LD with real GBP prices and stock. No invented reviews, identifiers or product photos.
- Crawlable card links, 24-card catalogue pages, a dynamic sitemap and robots file. Preview and checkout-return pages are noindex.
- English language declaration, image descriptions, eager loading for the main card image, branded social image and favicon.
- Confirmed TikTok profile linked in the footer and store structured data.

## Google account steps still required

1. Verify `togacards.co.uk` in [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). Domain verification needs DNS access; URL-prefix verification offers alternative methods.
2. Submit `https://togacards.co.uk/sitemap.xml`, inspect the homepage/shop/a card URL and request indexing. Review indexing and search queries weekly. Deployment does not establish indexing or rankings.
3. Validate a photographed card URL using [Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results). Add delivery/returns information only from real business policies.
4. Set up [Merchant Center](https://merchants.google.com/) for free product listings, with accurate photos, delivery, returns and product data. Merchant verification and product submission are separate from website structured data.
5. Confirm visible business contact, delivery, returns and privacy details, real photos and working checkout before paying for traffic.

Sources: [Google ecommerce navigation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/help-google-understand-your-ecommerce-site-structure), [Product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet), [Free product listings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13889434?hl=en-GB).

## Recommended promotion order

1. **Google Search and free Shopping listings** for people searching for particular players, card numbers and sets.
2. **Facebook Page and relevant UK football-card groups** for singles and set completion. Follow each group's rules. Start with a useful weekly restock post and answer wanted-card requests where allowed.
3. **TikTok @toga.cards**, optionally reusing original clips on Instagram Reels. Start with three 15–30-second videos weekly: card close-ups, missing-number searches, condition explanations and packing orders.
4. **TikTok Shop pilot** after seller verification and category confirmation. Trial 5–10 identified singles or fully described bundles with separate stock allocations.

The ranking above is a practical recommendation, not a forecast of sales. No Facebook account, social post, advertising campaign or TikTok Shop has been created by this work.

Use tracking links such as `https://togacards.co.uk/shop?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=launch`. UTM parameters keep the clean canonical but do not install analytics. Decide tracking/consent behavior before adding pixels.

## TikTok Shop findings

The current [UK Restricted Products Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3846773802092295&lang=en-GB), section 4.11.4, labels second-hand ungraded collectible cards **non-invite only**, requiring:

- “Second-hand”, “pre-owned” or “pre-loved” in titles and descriptions.
- At least two images; front and back is a practical choice.
- Accurate condition descriptions.

The same policy has a conflicting broader invite-only heading in section 4.11.3, and the [Invite-only policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?from=policy&identity=1&knowledge_id=7753820805875458&role=1) also refers broadly to collectible cards. Confirm the exact ungraded-card category in Seller Center before relying on eligibility. Graded cards have additional approval/grading requirements. A TikTok profile alone is not an approved Shop seller account.

Sign in to [UK Seller Center](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/), select the truthful business type, complete seller verification/payout setup, configure warehouse/returns/shipping, link @toga.cards and confirm the category. The owner should provide identity, business and bank details directly. Check current fees and shipping costs there before calculating margins; no fee rate is assumed here. Start with fixed, explicitly identified products; random products and breaks involve additional policies.

The current website has explicit eBay quantity updates and **no TikTok inventory/order integration**. Do not offer the same last copy across all three channels without reserving stock or implementing reliable reconciliation.

Measure product visits, baskets, purchases, order value and contribution after postage/platform fees. Evaluate over several weeks; views alone do not justify paid advertising.
